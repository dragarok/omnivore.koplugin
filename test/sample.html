Reading later, reading better

Most of what we save is never read. The trick is not to save less, but to make the reading part effortless: a quiet device, a clean page, and no notifications.

An e-reader is that device. Pages turn when you want them to, the screen doesn’t glow, and the article stays where you left it. Read it later; read it on paper-like glass.

Read it later. Read it later. Read it later, but do read it.

Kaizen (改善, "improvement") applies here too: small changes compound. A 5 % improvement every week is a 13× improvement in a year.

  • Save from anywhere.
  • Read on the e-reader.
  • Highlight what matters — and keep it.
